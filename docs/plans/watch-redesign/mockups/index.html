Voxglass on Apple Watch — redesign

Built around one rule: raise your wrist, keep listening. The watch is for continuing a book on a walk or run, or in bed, not for browsing a library. The player is one fixed screen made for spoken audio: skip back 15 s and forward 30 s, chapters, speed, a sleep timer, and the Crown for volume. The written spec is ../DESIGN.md. It uses the same Watch Listening Kit as Platterhead; only the accent colour (gold) and the audiobook controls differ. Frames are a 45 mm watch (198 × 242 pt).

2 · Player — made for spoken audio

Replaces the "combined book + now playing" list, where the transport sat between metadata and chapters and scrolled away. The face is fixed and doesn't scroll, the Crown is volume, and the cover colours tint the background. The side buttons skip 15 s back / 30 s forward, which is what audiobook listeners use; chapter jumps move to the chapter list. The toolbar holds Chapters, Speed, Sleep and Output.

5 · Honest states — the same Problem Card as Platterhead

Replaces the red caption line and the "Retry" text button. The card takes the transport's place, so you keep the title and chapter, and it shows the diagnostic code. "Playback stalled (timeControlStatu…)" truncated to two lines is the failure you hit on device; this is its replacement.
