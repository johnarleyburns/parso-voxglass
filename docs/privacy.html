Voxglass Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Summary

Voxglass has no app accounts, advertising, analytics, or tracking. We do not sell or share your personal data.

Network access

Voxglass contacts LibriVox, Project Gutenberg, and Internet Archive only when you search their catalogs or choose content to download or play. Internet Archive serves the LibriVox audio and associated artwork. These providers receive the ordinary network information necessary to answer your request, such as your IP address and request details, under their own privacy policies.

Your library and iCloud Sync

Your library, listening history, bookmarks, favorites, playback position, imported files, and cache are stored locally on your device. iCloud Sync is optional and can be turned off at any time in Voxglass Settings. When enabled, your library metadata, favorites, bookmarks, and playback position are stored only in your private iCloud account so that your own devices can stay in sync. Voxglass does not operate an account service or receive this private iCloud data.

Retention and your choices

Local data remains on your device until you delete it, remove the app, or clear the relevant content. You can turn off iCloud Sync at any time. To remove iCloud-synced data, delete the relevant library items or manage your private iCloud storage through Apple. Apple’s handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple’s privacy policy.

Contact

For privacy questions, open the Voxglass project’s support page.
