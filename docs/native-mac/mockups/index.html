VOXGLASS / NATIVE MAC PROPOSAL

A calm Mac workspace for making an audiobook.

New mockups for a new native macOS target. They are based on the current project store, narration flow, audio protocol, sync seams, and export pipeline. They do not revive the deferred Studio screens.

Design status: proposed only. The current shipped app remains iPhone/iPad plus Catalyst, Watch, and CarPlay.
01One front door

Sidebar destinations stay task-based: Listen, My Books, Discover, Narration.

02One work unit

A selected paragraph is the center of recording, review, notes, and playback.

03Details on demand

Inspectors and disclosures carry sync, storage, validation, and route complexity.

04Same work everywhere

The Mac adds a native workspace; it never becomes a prerequisite or a handoff.
