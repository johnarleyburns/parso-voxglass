# Content rights evidence

Release records contain the provider-rights research for the catalog sources used by Voxglass. The app limits its remote catalog and download experience to LibriVox recordings and Project Gutenberg texts; LibriVox audio is delivered through the Internet Archive's LibriVox collection.

## Evidence reviewed

- LibriVox states that its volunteer recordings are donated to the public domain: <https://librivox.org/pages/public-domain/>.
- Project Gutenberg publishes the terms applicable to each ebook and its Project Gutenberg license: <https://www.gutenberg.org/policy/license>.
- Internet Archive states that it does not guarantee an item's rights status. Voxglass therefore limits retrieval to the LibriVox collection rather than treating Internet Archive generally as a rights clearance source: <https://archivesupport.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/360014759692-Rights>.

## Release control

Before a release, retain the dated provider pages above, verify that the app’s catalog filters still limit remote content to the stated sources, and retain any item-specific provenance or takedown records with the release record. This is evidence of the review process, not a substitute for legal advice or for territory-specific rights review.
