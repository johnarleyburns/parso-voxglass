Storage & iCloud

Back up now
Nothing local is ever removed before the iCloud copy is verified.

A recording is only eligible to be offloaded once its iCloud upload has been checked byte-for-byte by SHA-256 and the remote asset id is written into the project database. A matching file size is not sufficient and is not accepted.

Two separate budgets

The audiobooks you listen to and the audiobooks you are making never share a cache. Conflating them would let a narration project evict your offline listening library, or the reverse.

Every full-capability device is a peer writer — no device is authoritative, and there is no ownership or lock to be caught out by. The watch is a companion to the iPhone and never touches iCloud.

mac-09 · Storage & iCloud (§6) Everything here is Core and shared with iOS — asset states, the offload rule, the eviction planner and executor — so the Mac gets iCloud offload without reimplementing it. What is genuinely Mac-specific is one number: a per-device cache limit that defaults to 50 GB rather than 10 GB, because it is never synced.
