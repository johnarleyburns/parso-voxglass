App Intents · Live Activity · Siri

Voxglass already owns the lock-screen MPNowPlayingInfoCenter surface. This layer adds the three things users actually ask an audiobook app for: a persistent Live Activity with a real progress bar and skip buttons, Siri phrases that work without opening the app (“resume my audiobook”, “sleep timer 20 minutes”), and App Intents that show up in Shortcuts, Spotlight, the Action button, widgets and Control Center.

Widgets and controls are thin wrappers over the same intents, so there is exactly one place where “resume the right book at the right offset” is implemented.
One rule holds all of this together: every surface here mutates playback only through App Intents, and every intent resolves position through the existing PlaybackCoordinator + PositionStore path. Nothing gets a second, divergent notion of “where you were” — which is the constraint the release plan already treats as non-negotiable.
