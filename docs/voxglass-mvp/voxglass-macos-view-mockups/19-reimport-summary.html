What happens on Apply

Reused paragraphs keep their IDs, takes, notes, and review states. New paragraphs are inserted in document order. Retired paragraphs are hidden from the script but keep their rows and audio. Global paragraph numbers are recomputed once, so “¶ 218 of 2,884” stays correct everywhere. This step is not undoable — review it before applying.
