Voxglass Privacy Policy

Voxglass (bundle id guru.parso.voxglass) · Parso Consulting · Last updated November 4, 2026

Voxglass has no accounts, no tracking, and no analytics. Nothing you listen to or read leaves your device.

What Voxglass does

Voxglass is a free, open-source audiobook player for the public-domain LibriVox catalog, with companion apps for Apple Watch and CarPlay. Its "Read with AI" feature can also import a public-domain ebook and read it aloud using on-device text-to-speech, for books that have no human narration.

What stays on your device

  • Your library, playback positions, bookmarks, favorites, and listening history are stored in a local database on your device.
  • Audio you stream is kept in a cache so recently played books keep working offline. The cache is managed automatically — the oldest, least-used audio is evicted first when space is needed.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn it on, your library and listening progress sync through your own iCloud account via Apple's CloudKit — never a Voxglass server, because none exists.

Who Voxglass talks to — only when a feature you use needs it

  • The Internet Archive (archive.org, LibriVox's host) — to search, browse, and stream the audiobooks and cover art you ask for.
  • Project Gutenberg, via its public gutendex.com catalog API — only when you use Read with AI to import a book. The book's text is downloaded to your device and read aloud entirely on-device; it is never sent anywhere else.

No ads, no analytics, no tracking

Voxglass contains no advertising SDKs, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking of any kind. It uses only Apple-provided frameworks.

Beta testing (TestFlight)

If you test Voxglass through Apple's TestFlight and choose to share crash reports with developers (an iOS setting you control), Apple may pass us a sanitized crash log so we can fix the bug. This is Apple's own standard TestFlight feature — Voxglass includes no separate analytics or crash-reporting code of its own, and this only applies during beta testing, never to the released App Store app.

Children's privacy

Voxglass does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because it does not collect personal information from anyone.

Open source

Voxglass's complete source code is public, under the GNU General Public License v3.0 or later with its own App Store distribution exception (see the LICENSE file), at github.com/johnarleyburns/parso-voxglass.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change and the new version will be posted both here and in the app.
