# Court-opinion PDF fixtures

- `supreme-court-hewitt.pdf` is the official Supreme Court slip opinion in
  *Hewitt v. United States*, No. 23-1002, downloaded from
  https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/24pdf/23-1002_1p24.pdf.
- `tax-court-jm-assets.pdf` is the official United States Tax Court Reports
  pamphlet containing *JM Assets, LP v. Commissioner*, 165 T.C. 1 (2025),
  downloaded from https://ustaxcourt.gov/files/documents/165_TC_1-36.pdf.

They are retained as real-world, selectable-text PDF regression fixtures for
the Read with AI importer.
