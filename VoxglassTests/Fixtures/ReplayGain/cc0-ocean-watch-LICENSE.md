# Watch AAC conversion fixture

`cc0-ocean-watch.mp3` is the one-second, 128 kbps MP3 derivative of the bundled
Platterhead CC0 recording “Ocean Waves” by Nox_Sound (Freesound 829629), copied from
`parso-tonearm/Tests/Fixtures/cc0-ocean-jamendo.mp3`. Original source:
`parso-tonearm/Resources/Audio/ambient-ocean.wav`.

Used offline to test extensionless cached MP3 → 96 kbps AAC conversion and durable
watch installation. Phone originals and narration/export masters are unchanged.
